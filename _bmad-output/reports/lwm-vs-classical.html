Frozen LWM v1.1 vs classical ML

Tor Vergata Wi-Fi CSI · 4 activities (empty, sitting, standing, walking) · 4500 LWM runs and 365 classical runs · commit 12db35a

Within a room, frozen LWM wins. Its best configurations reach 100 test macro-F1, and a validation-selected one scores about 98, against about 90–96 for classical features.

Across rooms, LWM's best runs beat classical's, but its typical run does not. The best LWM configurations reach about 58–60 F1, against 49–56 for classical. Hundreds of LWM configurations score a perfect validation F1, though, and their median on the unseen room is about 20 (chance is 25). The classical median is 40–44. Validation in the training room cannot pick out the LWM configurations that transfer.

Reading the results

  • Within room: LWM is better on every protocol, in both best case and typical, and clearly so on mixed (98.23 vs 90.19 typical). On r1 and r2 the intervals overlap.
  • Cross room, best case: LWM leads by about 2 points on r1→r2 with heavily overlapping intervals, and by about 11 on r2→r1. LWM's many more runs inflate its best case.
  • Cross room, typical: classical is about twice as good (40–44 vs about 20). With frozen LWM features, validation-selected models fall to chance on the unseen room.
  • Setups that transfer best: 2 antennas, global rescale with amplitude-only or phase sanitising, mean+std pooling, and 40–50 packet windows. This is a lead to test with room-aware validation, not a validated result.

Setup

  • LWM: frozen LWM v1.1 per-packet embeddings (cls or channel_mean, 128-d), pooled per window by mean or mean_std. Heads: logreg, knn, mlp, random_forest, svm. Swept over 2/4 antennas, 5 input presets, and window lengths 1–50.
  • Classical: amplitude_stats, paper_beamforming or both, with or without mRMR feature selection. Heads: knn, random_forest, svm.
  • Protocols: time-block split within r1, r2 and both rooms. Room hold-out trains on one room and tests on the other.
  • Selection: each head tunes its hyper-parameters on validation macro-F1, refits on train+val, and is scored once on test. One seed.
  • Most LWM runs were recorded with a dirty working tree: the untracked sweep log only, the code was at commit 12db35a. They are included here.
